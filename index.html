Exhibition catalogueSix works · WebGL · 2026

Real-time 3D, built in parallel

Each work was made by a separate AI subagent, all at the same time, then tested in a browser. Every piece tests a different part of 3D: scene graphs, shaders, physics, character rigs, particles and materials. Each also has its own interface, in a different design language. Open any plate to explore it. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
